— About Premiere Week

Where Northwestern's Year
in Film Comes to Life.

Every spring, Northwestern's Radio, Television, Video, and Film (RTVF) department culminates in a week-long celebration of student film. Premiere Week is that celebration — a curated showcase of every project made across courses and student organizations throughout the academic year.

"The moment students stop making films for class and start making them for an audience."

Screenings are open to the Northwestern community. Each film is introduced by its director, followed by a post-screening discussion. It's the closest thing we have to a proper premiere — and it shows.
